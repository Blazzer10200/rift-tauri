Color & Accent

Dark-only, true-neutral graphite (Graphite Ink). The accent ramp is driven by three independent dials — hue, vividness, and strength — all exposed in Settings. Status and activity colors are accent-independent and never recolor with the theme. Solid accent is rare — reserved for the primary action; everything else is a ghost wash.

Accent — 3-dial system
Hue
--accent-h
163 (emerald default)
The color angle on the oklch wheel. Changing this re-themes the entire accent ramp from ghost fills to the solid Send button.
Vividness (chroma)
--accent-c
0.15 (default)
Chroma of the accent ramp. Lower = more grey and neutral; higher = saturated and vivid. Controls how much color bleeds into ghost fills.
Strength (alpha)
--accent-a
0.12 (default)
Alpha of ghost fills (--accent-soft). The ghost-border and ring derive from multiples of this value (×2.9 and ×3.75).

--accent-fg is the dark ink used on top of solid accent. --accent-soft is the ghost fill (alpha = --accent-a); --ghost-border = ×2.9; --ring = ×3.75.

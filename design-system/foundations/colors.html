Color & Accent

Dark-only, true-neutral graphite (Graphite Ink). One themeable hue (--accent-h, emerald 163 by default) drives the entire accent ramp; status colors are accent-independent and never recolor with the theme. Solid accent is rare — reserved for the primary action; everything else is a ghost wash that only colors on hover.
